# Iteration

Sketchpad 5 produced `tests/fixtures/point_translation_iteration_native.gsp` from a free point and its direct polar translation. `tests/fixtures/point_translation_iteration_port.gsp` and `point_translation_iteration_depth5_port.gsp` were written by this port. Both generated files opened and saved byte-identically in Sketchpad 5. The depth-5 native view shows five additional image points beyond the first translated point.

The bounded case uses two object groups after the seed and first image. Code 76 has `07d0 07d2 090a 07d7`: the `07d2` references are `[seed, first_image, first_image]`, and the fixed depth is the 32-bit word at offset 16 of the 24-byte `090a` payload. Code 77 has `07d0 07d2 07d7` and references `[first_image, iteration_group]`. The group templates in `assets/point_iteration_group.bin` and `assets/point_iteration_binding_group.bin` came from the native fixture. The port changes only references, depth, and visibility.

The reader accepts this structure when the seed is a free point and the first image is its direct polar or fixed rectangular translation. It evaluates repeated images from the live seed and translation displacement, so moving the seed updates the visible sequence. The desktop Transform menu accepts the two selected points and a depth from 1 to 128. Additional mappings, carried shapes, and branch iterations still need separate record and geometry work.

## Rotated point mapping

The code-76 point-only iteration also works when its image is a fixed-angle rotation of the seed about an independent free center. The importer and desktop Iterate command accept this mapping. The evaluator uses each repeated angle about the current center rather than extrapolating the first displacement. The repeated points therefore stay on the seed's current orbit when either the seed or center moves.

`tests/replay_rotated_point_iteration.txt` creates a 60° image, adds a depth-4 point iteration, and moves the center. Both generated files opened and saved byte-identically in native Sketchpad 5. The visible repeated points formed the expected orbit before and after the move. Tests cover the rotation geometry, copied center references, and deletion of the center with its dependent image, iteration, and binding. Carried rotated shapes and marked-angle mappings are described below.

The rotation mapping also supports carrying the connecting segment. Code 76 and its two code-77 bindings use the same reference layout as a translated carried segment. The port rotates both endpoints for each repeated edge; the segment chain meets the iterated points and retains its edge lengths. `tests/replay_rotated_segment_iteration.txt` generates a 60° depth-4 chain and moves the center. Native Sketchpad displayed the expected rotated chain in both files and saved them byte-identically. Tests cover seed and center edits, shared endpoints, edge lengths, copy/paste remapping, and deletion dependencies.

## Parameter-controlled numeric depth

Its references are `[n, seed, parameter, image, calculation, calculation, image, label]`, with the same two-seed `090a` definition and code-77/code-92 children. Fixed and parameter-controlled numeric depths share `IterationDepth` and the same point/value evaluator. The depth parameter participates in dependency remapping and deletion. Selecting the five numeric-mapping objects plus a separate scalar parameter creates this form, regardless of selection order. Changing the depth immediately changes the repeated points, labels, and label hit targets; selected numeric labels highlight with their iteration. Scalar live depth is truncated to a whole number from 1 to 128 for creation and evaluation. Unsupported depth values produce no additional images while preserving the saved mapping.

`tests/replay_point_parameter_iteration_parameter_depth.txt` creates depth 4, changes `n` to 6 and `a` to 2, moves the seed, and separately edits the native depth-5 example to depth 3. Tests cover the native roundtrip, translated geometry, value sequence, live depth edits, two-parameter selection, label hit testing under zoom/pan, complete clipboard records, depth-parent remapping, and depth-parent deletion.

The generated depth-4 sketch, its depth-6/value-2/seed-moved edit, and the native fixture edited to depth 3 all opened and saved byte-identically in Sketchpad 5. The SVG export of the depth-6 edit contains the first image label 3.00 and repeated labels 4.00 through 9.00.

The native numeric fixtures use zero decimal places for the calculation, whereas generated calculations use two. ## Two translation branches carrying a triangle

The sibling `原象点初象携带多边形双映射深度4迭代.gsp` is preserved as `branch_polygon_iteration_native.gsp`. Its code-89 references are `[depth_parameter, seed, first_image, second_image, second_image, first_image, polygon, sides...]`. The `090a` definition has one seed, two mappings, and the cached depth at payload offset 16. Four code-77 bindings carry the interior and its three sides. The port imports all 13 objects, preserving the native bytes. Fixed depth uses code 76 and omits the leading depth parameter; a generated fixed-depth file is also supported.

The two images must be direct polar or rectangular translations of the free seed, and the triangular interior uses those three vertices. Selected sides are optional. Each branch substitutes the same seed with one of its two images. Translation paths commute, so a level-k image reached by j second mappings has offset `(k-j)*first_delta + j*second_delta` and multiplicity `choose(k,j)`. Levels 1 through n are the added images; the original interior is drawn separately. At depth 2 native Sketchpad shows six distinct triangles including the original, with the middle upper triangle darker because two paths reach it. At depth 4 the port stores 14 added offsets representing 30 paths.

The renderer combines repeated fills as `1-(1-alpha)^multiplicity`. The saved polygon opacity is read from header bytes 16–17 and travels with its color through copy/paste and color edits. Binding headers retain their own polygon fill or side stroke style. Desktop and SVG render the carried bindings, with no extra copied point markers. Their visible geometry supplies hit targets; selecting the iteration highlights its carried children.

`tests/replay_branch_polygon_iteration.txt` creates a parameter-controlled depth-3 triangle, changes n to 2 and moves the seed, edits the original native fixture to depth 2, and generates a fixed-depth-2 version. The generated depth-3, edited depth-2, and fixed-depth-2 files opened in Sketchpad 5 with the expected lattice and darker middle overlap and saved byte-identically. The edited native fixture is byte-identical to the depth-2 file opened and saved during the native record investigation. Tests cover path counts and multiplicities, transformed vertices and sides, complete clipboard records and remapping, depth-parent deletion, fixed depth, invalid mappings, the depth-128 storage bound, desktop selection/hit testing, and SVG primitive counts and overlap opacity.

Other carried polygons, more than two mappings, non-translation branches, and multiple point seeds remain pending.

## Numeric mapping with external formula inputs

Point/parameter iteration now accepts a scalar formula with two to sixteen inputs when the mapped parameter appears directly in its source list, at any index. Every reference to that parameter receives the previous step’s value. Other sources retain their current evaluated values during the iteration, including external calculations that depend on the mapped parameter. This follows native behavior rather than recursively reevaluating the entire external dependency graph.

`tests/replay_numeric_iteration_multiple_sources.txt` generates five native-verified files. With x=1 and k=2, `k+x` shows the first label 3 and repeated labels 5, 7, 9, 11; after k changes to 3, it shows 4, 7, 10, 13, 16. A compound measured-length ratio plus x advances by one, with a separate parameter controlling depth. The chained `(2*x)+x` case shows 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, confirming that the external `2*x` result stays fixed at 2. All five files open and save byte-identically in native Sketchpad 5; captures were inspected before save.

The group and binding layouts are unchanged. The importer and save validation accept the mapped parameter at any formula input position. Evaluation substitutes all occurrences of the mapped seed, applies the saved display precision without rounding the iteration state, and suppresses labels when an external measurement becomes undefined. The desktop Iterate selection finds the selected parameter among all formula sources. Regressions verify the native files and sequences, duplicate source substitution, clipboard remapping, deletion cascades, restored valid measurements, and selecting a mapped parameter that is the second formula input.

## Dilated point mappings and carried geometry

The existing code-76/code-89 iteration definitions accept a directly dilated image without new record templates. Fixed dilation uses the saved factor; marked-ratio dilation reads the current signed projection from its origin/base/target points. Centers and ratio-defining inputs may be supported constructed points. Independent inputs remain pinned within the sequence; direct seed references change at each step. With independent inputs, the repeated geometry follows powers of the fixed ratio about its center. Carried segments and rays transform both endpoints; carried circles transform their center and through-point, scaling the radius at each step. Negative factors alternate sides; an undefined marked ratio suppresses geometry and becomes live again when restored.

`tests/replay_dilated_iteration.txt` generates point contraction, negative-factor segments before/after moving seed and center, shrinking circles with fractional live depth, marked-ratio rays before/after changing ratio and depth, and a point/parameter mapping. The numeric mapping retains its independent calculator sequence (4, 8, 16, 32) while the positions expand by powers of 1.3. Native files are saved byte-identically and captures are inspected. Tests cover import, save/reopen, geometric powers, shrinking radii, numeric labels, source edits, copied dependency/binding closure, ratio degeneracy and recovery, deletion, and desktop selection of a dilated mapping. Other mapping forms remain pending.

## Reflection and vector translation mappings

Existing iteration and binding templates also accept directly reflected and vector-translated image points. A reflection uses the current external mirror and alternates between the seed and reflected image. Both endpoints of carried geometry follow the mapping; reflection preserves circle radii. Mirrors remain external inputs, including a line whose midpoint depends on the seed. Moving source or mirror inputs refreshes the whole sequence.

A vector image has three direct point references: source, start, and end. Every direct occurrence of the mapped seed is substituted. A seed also used as vector start makes the next point stationary at the external endpoint; a seed used as vector endpoint doubles displacement around the external start. Independent vectors use a constant offset. An external midpoint that depends on the seed remains pinned during the sequence, as verified by the native 160, 180, 200 positions. The shared orbit evaluator substitutes these inputs at each step, including both endpoints of carried shapes. Degenerate or non-finite repeated circles are omitted, matching ordinary circle validity.

`tests/replay_reflection_vector_iteration.txt` reproduces reflected points/segments/circles and a numeric mapping, an edited mirror, independent/shared-seed vectors, external midpoint dependencies, a dependent external mirror, expanding circles, vector rays before/after changing vector/depth, and collapsed circles. Native captures show stationary shared-start points, doubling shared-end points, frozen external inputs, alternating reflection geometry, expanding radii, and no repeated outline for collapsed circles. Regression coverage includes exact imports and generated edits, copy/paste of repeated references and midpoint hosts, numeric labels, source edits, mirror degeneracy/recovery, deletion, desktop selection, and SVG omission of zero-radius circles.

## Marked translations, angles, and shared transformation inputs

Point, segment, ray, circle, and combined point/parameter iterations now accept marked polar distances, two marked rectangular distances, either mixed fixed/marked rectangular translation, and a rotation defined by three marked angle points. Length measurements and length-valued calculations provide the translation distances. Their current values remain fixed within each sequence, even when their geometry depends indirectly on the seed. Moving their sources recomputes the whole sequence. Undefined values suppress dependent geometry until restored.

A single orbit evaluator handles all supported direct point mappings. It substitutes every direct occurrence of the seed in the transformation's point inputs. An external construction is pinned at its current document value for the entire sequence. Carried geometry uses consecutive orbit points as endpoints, preserving adjacency when angles or ratios change at each step. Repeated circles use those endpoints as center and through-point and omit zero radii.

`tests/replay_marked_iteration.txt` reproduces twelve native probes:

- A marked distance dependent on the seed stays at 20 pixels per step, then changes to 30 after editing its other endpoint.
- Two marked rectangular distances carry a segment; both mixed translations carry a circle or a live-depth ray.
- Reusing the seed as the first marked angle point makes subsequent images stationary. Reusing it as the last angle point produces successively changing rotation angles.
- A midpoint-defined rotation or dilation center remains pinned, even when its midpoint depends on the seed.
- Reusing the seed as marked-ratio target changes the ratio on every step: repeated x positions are 140.96, 116.777216, and 102.81474976710656.
- A length calculation drives a translation while a separate parameter formula generates labels 4, 8, and 16; editing the measured source moves all labels without changing their values.

All twelve generated originals/edits open and save byte-identically in Sketchpad 5, and native captures show the corresponding geometry and labels. Regression tests cover import/save, source edits, fractional depth, direct repeated references, carried adjacency and radii, undefined angle/ratio/calculation recovery, copied dependency references, deletion, and desktop selection. Iteration remains limited to one free seed, a supported directly transformed image, depth 1–128, and the supported carried shape or numeric mapping. Multiple independent seeds and additional branch constructions remain pending.

## Measured numeric angles

Code-29 point rotations and their supported iteration forms now also accept three-point angle and arc-angle measurements as direct numeric inputs. These degree values follow geometry edits and remain pinned within each sequence. They use the same substitution rules as calculated angles and can carry supported segments, rays, circles, and numeric labels. See `dev/marked_angle_rotation_analysis.md` and `tests/replay_measured_angle_rotation.txt` for native creation evidence and original/edited segment and live-depth circle probes.
